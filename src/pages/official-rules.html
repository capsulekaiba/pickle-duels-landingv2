These Official Rules govern gift card giveaways run through the Pickle Duels app. By taking part you agree to these rules, which are binding and final on all matters relating to the promotion.

Sponsor

The sponsor of this promotion is Rally XP, the company that operates Pickle Duels ("we", "us"). You can contact us at hello@pickleduels.com.

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by Apple, Google, or by any of the brands whose gift cards are offered as prizes.

Eligibility

  • You must have a Pickle Duels account in good standing and meet the age requirement in our Terms of Service.
  • If you are under the age of majority where you live, a parent or guardian must claim any prize on your behalf.
  • Employees and contractors of the sponsor, and members of their immediate families and households, are not eligible.

Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

Promotion period

We will announce when each giveaway starts and ends in the Pickle Duels app. Points earned outside an announced giveaway period do not count toward that giveaway.

How to earn points

Points are earned by playing in the Pickle Duels app. The current ways to earn points, and any limits on them, are shown in the app.

No purchase is necessary to take part or to win. The Pickle Duels app is free, and paying for anything will not improve your chances.

How prizes are awarded

How prizes are awarded in each giveaway, including how many points a prize requires or how winners are chosen, is explained in the app when the giveaway is announced. Prizes are available while supplies last, and we may limit how many prizes one person can win.

Odds of winning

Where winners are chosen by drawing, the odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received. Where prizes are redeemed with points, they are available while supplies last.

Winner notification and delivery

We will notify winners in the app or by email to the address on their account. The notification will explain how to claim the prize and the deadline to respond. If a winner does not respond in time, the prize may be forfeited and awarded to another eligible player.

We may ask a winner for the information needed to deliver the prize, and to confirm that they are eligible, before the prize is released.

Taxes

Any taxes on a prize are the winner's responsibility.

Conduct and disqualification

The fair play rules in our Terms of Service apply to this promotion. We may disqualify any entrant and void their points where we believe they have reported matches that were not played, colluded, used more than one account, or otherwise manipulated results or points. Our determination is final.

Publicity

We will not use a winner's name or likeness in promotional material without their permission.

Limitation of liability

By taking part, you release the sponsor from liability for any loss or injury arising from the promotion or from accepting or using a prize, to the fullest extent the law allows. We are not responsible for technical failures, or for points or entries that are lost, delayed, or not recorded. The limitation of liability in our Terms of Service also applies.

Privacy

Information you give us in connection with this promotion is handled under our Privacy Policy. We use information a winner provides only to confirm eligibility and deliver the prize.

Changes and cancellation

We may modify, suspend, or cancel the promotion if it cannot run as planned, for example because of fraud, technical problems, or legal requirements. If we do, we will announce it in the app.

Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws of the State of Washington, United States, and any dispute about the promotion will be resolved as described in our Terms of Service.
