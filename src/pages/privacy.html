Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are and what this covers

This policy applies to three things we operate:

  • This website — our marketing site.
  • The Pickle Duels app — our player app for iOS and Android.
  • VenueOS — our pickleball tournament management software for clubs and organizers, at venue.pickleduels.com.

Pickle Duels is operated by Rally XP, which is responsible for the personal information described here.

This website

We do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking on this website. The site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and loads no scripts, fonts, or images from other companies. Browsing these pages involves no tracking of any kind.

If you request a demo

The demo request form on our Organize page is the only place this website collects personal information. When you submit it we receive:

What we collectWhy
Your nameTo address you when we reply
Your email addressTo contact you about your demo request
Your club or organizationTo understand who we would be working with
Your roleTo tailor the demo to what you do
Approximate events per yearTo understand the scale of your events
Your message (optional)Anything you choose to tell us

We use these details only to respond to your request and to talk with you about VenueOS. We do not add you to a marketing list without your agreement, and we do not sell or share them. Submissions are processed and stored by Web3Forms, who deliver them to us by email. We keep demo requests only as long as we need them to respond and follow up with you.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes technical information needed to deliver pages to you, including your IP address and browser type. Netlify also serves a small script of its own on our pages. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors.

The Pickle Duels app

When you sign up for Pickle Duels, we collect your name and your email address. We use that information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Support in-app gameplay features such as matches and user progress
  • Communicate with you if needed, for example about a support question

As you play, the app also records gameplay activity such as match results, scores, and leaderboard standings. Leaderboard standings may be visible to other players.

When you choose to scan a QR code, the app asks for permission to use your device's camera. You can turn camera access on or off at any time in your device settings.

If you redeem a reward or discount, we may share the information needed to redeem it with the business providing that offer.

Signing in

We use third-party services to let you log in, including Apple, Google, and Facebook. These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We do not control how these third parties use your data.

Prizes

If you win a prize, we may ask for additional information needed to deliver it, such as a shipping address. We use that information only to fulfil your prize. See our Official Rules & Prize Terms for details.

VenueOS

VenueOS is used by clubs and tournament organizers. Two different groups of people have information in it, and our role differs for each.

If you are an organizer

When you create a VenueOS account we collect the information needed to set it up and identify you. Accounts are secured through Clerk, our authentication provider, who processes your sign-in credentials on our behalf. You can sign in with an email address and password, or with Google or Facebook.

If you registered for a tournament

When you sign up for a tournament run on VenueOS, the organizer collects your registration details — including your name and email address — and we store and process that information on their behalf. The organizer decides what to collect and how to use it. If you want your registration information corrected or removed, contact the club or organizer running the event, or contact us and we will help put you in touch.

Emails about future tournaments

VenueOS lets organizers email people who registered for their past tournaments to invite them to upcoming ones, using the email addresses collected at registration. These emails come from the organizer running the event, not from us. If you no longer want to receive them, contact the organizer, or email us at hello@pickleduels.com and we will help.

Data security

We take appropriate measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children

We do not collect data from users under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete that information.

Your rights and choices

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us at hello@pickleduels.com. If your information is in VenueOS because you registered for a tournament, see If you registered for a tournament above.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. To exercise any of them, email us and we will respond as the law requires.
